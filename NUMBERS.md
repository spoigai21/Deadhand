**What this decides:** one matching decision against 50 candidate drivers costs about 6 ms of
routing. A day of ~400k decisions is about 40 minutes of serial routing, and the calls are
independent. **Route per pair; no hex-to-hex matrix.** Appendix A becomes a question about
matching radius, not a workaround.
